# 第 1 节来源核实记录

核实日期 2026-09-28。论文类来源用 PubMed E-utilities 取回题录与摘要全文，官方文件直接抓取网页正文；下面「原文」都是从摘要或政策文件正文里抄出的原句。出版社站点对脚本返回 403 的，注明用 PubMed 元数据核对。

## 1 戒烟
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMsa1211128> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 23343063 摘要）。标题 "21st-century hazards of smoking and benefits of cessation in the United States" 匹配。
  - 原文："The rate of death from any cause among current smokers was about three times that among those who had never smoked (hazard ratio for women, 3.0; 99% confidence interval [CI], 2.7 to 3.3; hazard ratio for men, 2.8; 99% CI, 2.4 to 3.1)."
  - 原文："Life expectancy was shortened by more than 10 years among the current smokers"；"Adults who had quit smoking at 25 to 34, 35 to 44, or 45 to 54 years of age gained about 10, 9, and 6 years of life"；"Cessation before the age of 40 years reduces the risk of death associated with continued smoking by about 90%."
- <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000146.pub5> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 29852054 摘要）。标题 "Nicotine replacement therapy versus control for smoking cessation" 匹配。
  - 原文："133 with 64,640 participants contributed to the primary comparison"；"The RR of abstinence for any form of NRT relative to control was 1.55 (95% confidence interval (CI) 1.49 to 1.61)"；"1.64 (95% CI 1.53 to 1.75, 51 trials, 25,754 participants) for nicotine patch"；"NRTs increase the rate of quitting by 50% to 60%."
- <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD006103.pub8> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 37142273 摘要）。标题 "Nicotine receptor partial agonists for smoking cessation" 匹配。
  - 原文："75 trials of 45,049 people"；"high-certainty evidence that varenicline helps more people to quit than placebo (RR 2.32, 95% CI 2.15 to 2.51; I2 = 60%, 41 studies, 17,395 participants)"；"cytisine helps more people to quit smoking than placebo (RR 1.30, 95% confidence interval (CI) 1.15 to 1.47)"；"people taking varenicline are more likely to report SAEs than those not taking it (RR 1.23, 95% CI 1.01 to 1.48)"。

## 2 血压
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 26724178 摘要）。标题 "Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis" 匹配。
  - 原文："We identified 123 studies with 613,815 participants for the tabular meta-analysis."
  - 原文："Every 10 mm Hg reduction in systolic blood pressure significantly reduced the risk of major cardiovascular disease events (relative risk [RR] 0·80, 95% CI 0·77-0·83), coronary heart disease (0·83, 0·78-0·88), stroke (0·73, 0·68-0·77), and heart failure (0·72, 0·67-0·78), which, in the populations studied, led to a significant 13% reduction in all-cause mortality (0·87, 0·84-0·91)."
  - 原文："β blockers were inferior to other drugs for the prevention of major cardiovascular disease events, stroke, and renal failure."

## 3 疫苗
- <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004876.pub4> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 29388197 摘要）。标题 "Vaccines for preventing influenza in the elderly" 匹配。
  - 原文："from 6% to 2.4% (risk ratio (RR) 0.42, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.27 to 0.66; low-certainty evidence)"；"from 6% to 3.5% (RR 0.59, 95% CI 0.47 to 0.73; moderate-certainty evidence)"；"30 people would need to be vaccinated to prevent one person experiencing influenza, and 42 would need to be vaccinated to prevent one person having an ILI."
  - 原文："providing very low-certainty evidence for the effect on mortality (RR 1.02, 95% CI 0.11 to 9.72)"
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1408544> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 25785969 摘要）。标题 "Polysaccharide conjugate vaccine against pneumococcal pneumonia in adults" 匹配。
  - 原文："community-acquired pneumonia occurred in 49 persons in the PCV13 group and 90 persons in the placebo group (vaccine efficacy, 45.6%; 95.2% confidence interval [CI], 21.8 to 62.5)"；"invasive pneumococcal disease occurred in 7 persons in the PCV13 group and 28 persons in the placebo group (vaccine efficacy, 75.0%; 95% CI, 41.4 to 90.8)"；"not in preventing community-acquired pneumonia from any cause"。
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1501184> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 25916341 摘要）。标题 "Efficacy of an adjuvanted herpes zoster subunit vaccine in older adults" 匹配。
  - 原文："A total of 15,411 participants"；"Overall vaccine efficacy against herpes zoster was 97.2% (95% confidence interval [CI], 93.7 to 99.0; P<0.001)."
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1917338> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 32997908 摘要）。标题 "HPV Vaccination and the Risk of Invasive Cervical Cancer" 匹配。

After additional adjustment for other covariates, the incidence rate ratio was 0.37 (95% CI, 0.21 to 0.57). After adjustment for all covariates, the incidence rate ratio was 0.12 (95% CI, 0.00 to 0.34) among women who had been vaccinated before the age of 17 years"。
- <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7075214.htm> — 已打开。标题 "国务院关于印发《国民健康"十五五"规划》的通知" 匹配。

## 4 行动不便者的运动
- <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-024-01072-9> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 38252359 摘要）。标题 "The role of physical activity in the association between disability and mortality among US older adults: a nationwide prospective cohort study" 匹配。
  - 原文："This analysis comprised 177,360 US participants (104,556 women), aged ≥60 years"；"Over a mean (SD) follow-up of 8.02 (5.43) years, 66,694 deaths occurred"。
  - 原文："Among people with disability in activities in daily living, those reaching physical activity recommendations had 25%, 24% and 33% lower risk of all-cause mortality, cardiovascular diseases, and cancer death, respectively"；"Values were 23%, 22% and 24% for those with disability in instrumental activities in daily living."
  - 原文："individuals with disability in activities of daily living or instrumental activities in daily living who meet the recommended physical activity had moderately higher mortality than those without disability who did not achieve the recommended physical activity."
- <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128> — 已打开。标题 "WHO guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour" 匹配。
  - 原文："recommendations for children, adolescents, adults and older adults on the amount of physical activity (frequency, intensity and duration) required to offer significant health benefits"；"recommendations are provided on the associations between sedentary behaviour and health outcomes, as well as for subpopulations, such as pregnant and postpartum women, and people living with chronic conditions or disability."
  - 未采用：指南正文 PDF 未下载，150 到 300 分钟的具体推荐没有写进条目，条目里的数字全部来自上一条队列研究。

## 5 久坐
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)30370-1> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 27475271 摘要）。标题 "Does physical activity attenuate, or even eliminate, the detrimental association of sitting time with mortality? A harmonised meta-analysis of data from more than 1 million men and women" 匹配。
  - 原文："These studies included 1 005 791 individuals who were followed up for 2-18·1 years, during which 84 609 (8·4%) died."；"there was no increased risk of mortality during follow-up in those who sat for more than 8 h/day but who also reported >35·5 MET-h per week of activity (HR=1·04; 95% CI 0·99-1·10)"；"HR=1·59, 1·52-1·66, for the lowest quartile of physical activity [<2·5 MET-h per week] and sitting >8 h/day"。
  - 原文："High levels of moderate intensity physical activity (ie, about 60-75 min per day) seem to eliminate the increased risk of death associated with high sitting time."；"Watching TV for 3 h or more per day was associated with increased mortality regardless of physical activity, except in the most active quartile."

## 6 癌症筛查
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2208375> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 36214590 摘要）。标题 "Effect of Colonoscopy Screening on Risks of Colorectal Cancer and Related Death" 匹配。
  - 原文："Follow-up data were available for 84,585 participants"；"28,220 in the invited group, 11,843 of whom (42.0%) underwent screening"。
  - 原文："the risk of colorectal cancer at 10 years was 0.98% in the invited group and 1.20% in the usual-care group, a risk reduction of 18% (risk ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.70 to 0.93). The risk of death from colorectal cancer was 0.28% in the invited group and 0.31% in the usual-care group (risk ratio, 0.90; 95% CI, 0.64 to 1.16). The risk of death from any cause was 11.03% in the invited group and 11.04% in the usual-care group (risk ratio, 0.99; 95% CI, 0.96 to 1.04)."
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(13)62218-7> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 24192252 摘要）。标题 "Efficacy of HPV-based screening for prevention of invasive cervical cancer: follow-up of four European randomised controlled trials" 匹配。
  - 原文："176,464 women aged 20-64 years were randomly assigned"；"The rate ratio for invasive cervical carcinoma among all women from recruitment to end of follow-up was 0·60 (95% CI 0·40-0·89)"。
  - 原文："Data of large-scale randomised trials support initiation of HPV-based screening from age 30 years and extension of screening intervals to at least 5 years."

## 未采用与说明
- 乳腺癌筛查（钼靶）没有进「收益」栏：这一版没有找到一条我能亲自打开的、能给出明确死亡率降幅的原始文献或官方文件，按「不确定的数字宁可不写」处理。第十五五规划把乳腺癌筛查列为国家行动，属于政策依据，不构成效果数字。
- 流感疫苗的死亡率结论在 Cochrane 综述里是「极低质量证据」，条目里按原样写明，没有把它写成「打了就不会死」。
