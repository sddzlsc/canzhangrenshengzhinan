# 第 3 节来源核实记录

核实日期 2026-09-28。论文来源用 PubMed E-utilities 取题录与摘要，官方来源直接抓网页正文。

## 1 心脏骤停（按压与 AED）
- <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamacardio.2017.0008> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 28297003 摘要）。标题 "Bystander Defibrillation for Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest in Public vs Residential Locations" 匹配。
  - 原文："this nationwide study identified 18 688 patients in Denmark with first-time OHCA from June 1, 2001, to December 31, 2012"；"Thirty-day survival after bystander defibrillation increased in public locations from 8.3% (95% CI, 1.5%-35.4%) in 2001/2002 to 57.5% (95% CI, 48.6%-66.0%) in 2011/2012"；"in residential locations, from 0.0% ... to 25.6% (95% CI, 14.6%-41.1%)"。
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1406038> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 26061836 摘要）。标题 "Mobile-phone dispatch of laypersons for CPR in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest" 匹配。
  - 原文："overall 9828 were recruited during the study"；"The rate of bystander-initiated CPR was 62% (188 of 305 patients) in the intervention group and 48% (172 of 360 patients) in the control group (absolute difference for intervention vs. control, 14 percentage points; 95% confidence interval, 6 to 21; P<0.001)."
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resuscitation.2025.110771> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 41117574 摘要）。标题 "European Resuscitation Council Guidelines 2025 Adult Basic Life Support" 匹配。这一条用于现场做法（按压、通气、AED 使用）的权威依据，条目里没有从它取数字。
- <https://www.jingyuan.gov.cn/zfxxgk/bmhxzxxgk/xzxxgk/bwz/fdzdgknr/yjxx/art/2023/art_3b0aa1760b8541c9b74da370e1e9c28a.html> — 已打开。标题 "牢记"12110"！关键时刻发短信能救命！" 匹配。
  - 原文："12110，为中国公安机关统一的公益性短信报警号码"；"公安部已于2012年在全国普遍开展12110的短信报警服务"；"不方便接语音报警的听力障碍人士、语言障碍人士"；报警短信要写"①案（事）件发生的时间 ②地点 ③简要内容 ④其他特殊需要说明的情况、诉求"。
  - 说明：这是甘肃省靖远县人民政府网站转载的公安宣传信息，属官方站点；12110 的号码在全国多地不一（部分地区需加区号），条目备注里提醒以当地公安公布为准。

## 2 卒中
- <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2016.13647> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 27673305 摘要）。标题 "Time to Treatment With Endovascular Thrombectomy and Outcomes From Ischemic Stroke: A Meta-analysis" 匹配。
  - 原文："Among all 1287 patients"；"The odds of better disability outcomes at 90 days ... cOR at 3 hours, 2.79 (95% CI, 1.96 to 3.98), absolute risk difference (ARD) for lower disability scores, 39.2%; cOR at 6 hours, 1.98 (95% CI, 1.30 to 3.00), ARD, 30.2%; cOR at 8 hours, 1.57 (95% CI, 0.86 to 2.88), ARD, 15.7%; retaining statistical significance through 7 hours and 18 minutes."
  - 原文："each 1-hour delay to reperfusion was associated with a less favorable degree of disability (cOR, 0.84 [95% CI, 0.76 to 0.93]) and less functional independence (OR, 0.81 [95% CI, 0.71 to 0.92], ARD, -5.2% [95% CI, -8.3% to -2.1%])."
  - 说明：溶栓 4.5 小时时间窗写在备注里，属于通行的临床口径，这一版没有单独核实到能打开的原始文献，故没有标数字来源。

## 3 癫痫
- <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/epilepsy> — 已打开。标题 "Epilepsy" 匹配。
  - 原文："Epilepsy is a chronic noncommunicable disease of the brain that affects around 50 million people worldwide."
  - 原文："It is estimated that up to 70% of people living with epilepsy could live seizure-free if properly diagnosed and treated."；"The risk of premature death in people with epilepsy is up to three times higher than for the general population."；"Three quarters of people with epilepsy living in low-income countries do not get the treatment they need."
  - 原文："Discontinuing antiseizure medicine can be considered after 2 years without seizures and should take into account relevant clinical, social and personal factors."
  - 说明：现场动作（护头、不按人、不塞东西、超过 5 分钟叫 120）是通行急救做法，属共识，条目备注里与数字分开写明。

## 4 气道异物
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resuscitation.2024.110258> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 38825222 摘要）。标题 "Evaluation of basic life support interventions for foreign body airway obstructions: A population-based cohort study" 匹配。
  - 原文："We identified 3,677 patient encounters, including 709 FBAOs requiring intervention."；"Bystanders and paramedics did not relieve the FBAO in 151 (23.5%) and 11 (16.7%) cases"；"FBAOs not relieved before paramedic arrival had a higher proportion of deaths (n = 4[0.4%] versus n = 92[42.4%], p < 0.001)."
  - 原文："AT and CC were associated with decreased odds of FBAO relief relative to BB (adjusted odds ratio [aOR] 0.49 [95%CI 0.30-0.80] and 0.14 [95%CI 0.07-0.28], respectively). CC were associated with decreased odds of survival to discharge (aOR 0.04 [95%CI 0.01-0.32]). AT, CC, and BB were implicated in intervention-associated injuries in four, nine, and zero cases, respectively."

## 5 低血糖
- <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1003795> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 20925543 摘要）。标题 "Severe Hypoglycemia and Risks of Vascular Events and Death" 匹配。
  - 原文："among 11,140 patients with type 2 diabetes"；"231 patients (2.1%) had at least one severe hypoglycemic episode; 150 had been assigned to intensive glucose control (2.7% of the 5571 patients in that group), and 81 had been assigned to standard glucose control (1.5% of the 5569 patients in that group)."
  - 原文："severe hypoglycemia was associated with a significant increase in the adjusted risks of major macrovascular events (hazard ratio, 2.88; 95% confidence interval [CI], 2.01 to 4.12), major microvascular events (hazard ratio, 1.81; 95% CI, 1.19 to 2.74), death from a cardiovascular cause (hazard ratio, 2.68; 95% CI, 1.72 to 4.19), and death from any cause (hazard ratio, 2.69; 95% CI, 1.97 to 3.67)."
  - 说明：15 到 20 克补糖、15 分钟后复测的具体剂量写在备注里，属通行做法；这一版没有核实到能打开的原始文献给出该剂量，故未标数字来源。

## 6 大出血与止血带
- <https://doi.org/10.1097/TA.0000000000001826> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 29432381 摘要）。标题 "Systematic review of prehospital tourniquet use in civilian limb trauma" 匹配。
  - 原文："Reports were included from six regional trauma centers and one interregional collaboration (total of 572 cases). One national prehospital database report was included but analyzed separately (2,048 cases). All were retrospective cohort studies without prospective data collection. Three reports defined a primary outcome, two had a nontourniquet control group, and no two articles reported the same variables."
  - 原文："Mortality was low, and limb-specific complications were infrequent but variably reported."；"The rapid increase in the civilian use of tourniquets for limb hemorrhage control has occurred without a large amount or high quality of data."

## 7 跌倒之后
- <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/falls> — 已打开。标题 "Falls" 匹配。
  - 原文："Falls are the second leading cause of unintentional injury deaths worldwide. Each year an estimated 684 000 individuals die from falls globally of which over 80% are in low- and middle-income countries. Adults older than 60 years of age suffer the greatest number of fatal falls. 37.3 million falls that are severe enough to require medical attention occur each year."
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.injury.2020.08.027> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 32907702 摘要）。标题 "Prognosis of nonoperative treatment in elderly patients with a hip fracture: A systematic review and meta-analysis" 匹配。
  - 原文："Pooled mortality rates after 30 days, six months and one year were 36%, 46%, and 60%, respectively. 33% of the patients developed in-hospital complications. At six months post trauma, 9.6% of the patients were able to mobilize."
  - 原文："Approximately two-thirds of the patients were managed nonoperatively due to medical reasons"（说明这是不适合手术的体弱老人，条目备注里写明了这个外推边界）。
