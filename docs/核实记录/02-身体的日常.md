## 1 压疮与减压坐垫
- <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2010.03168.x> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 21070197 摘要）。标题 "A randomized clinical trial on preventing pressure ulcers with wheelchair seat cushions" 匹配。
  - 原文："Two hundred thirty-two participants were recruited between June 2004 and May 2008"；"Twelve nursing homes"；"There were eight (6.7%) IT ulcers in the SFC group and one (0.9%) in the SPC group (P=.04). There were 21 (17.6%) combined IT and sacral ulcers in the SFC group and 12 (10.6%) in the SPC group (P=.14)."
  - 原文："All participants were provided with a fitted wheelchair"；"7.6-cm crosscut foam cushion"。

## 2 自主神经反射异常
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmr.2008.10.017> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 19345787 摘要）。标题 "A systematic review of the management of autonomic dysreflexia after spinal cord injury" 匹配。
  - 原文："Thirty-one studies were assessed, including 6 RCTs."；"The initial acute nonpharmacologic management of an episode of AD (ie, positioning the patient upright, loosening tight clothing, eliminating any precipitating stimulus) is supported by clinical consensus and physiologic data (level 5 evidence)."
  - 原文："The use of antihypertensive drugs in the presence of sustained elevated blood pressure is supported by level 1 (prazosin) and level 2 evidence (nifedipine and prostaglandin E(2))."；"Preventative strategies ... primarily were supported by level 4 (pre-post studies) and level 5 (observational studies) evidence."

## 3 辅具验配
- <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/assistive-technology> — 已打开。标题 "Assistive technology" 匹配。
  - 原文："Globally, more than 2.5 billion people need one or more assistive products. With an ageing global population and a rise in noncommunicable diseases, an estimated 3.5 billion people will need assistive technology by 2050."
  - 原文："Assistive products can range from physical products such as wheelchairs, glasses, prosthetic limbs, white canes, and hearing aids to digital solutions such as speech recognition or time management software and captioning."；"Most people who use assistive technology use more than one product, making integrated services important."
- <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240074521> — 已打开（页面元数据）。世界卫生组织 2023 年发布轮椅配置指南 "WHO releases new Wheelchair provision guidelines"，要求按人评估、配置、培训与随访。
  - 说明：指南正文 PDF 没有下载，条目里只写了它的做法口径（按人配置、培训、随访），没有引用具体数字。

## 4 助听器与认知
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01406-X> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 37478886 摘要）。标题 "Hearing intervention versus health education control to reduce cognitive decline in older adults with hearing loss in the USA (ACHIEVE)" 匹配。
  - 原文："we randomly assigned 977"；"3-year cognitive change (in SD units) was not significantly different between the hearing intervention and health education control groups (-0·200 [95% CI -0·256 to -0·144] in the hearing intervention group and -0·202 [-0·258 to -0·145] in the control group; difference 0·002 [-0·077 to 0·081]; p=0·96)."
  - 原文："a prespecified sensitivity analysis showed a significant difference in the effect of the hearing intervention on 3-year cognitive change between the ARIC and de novo cohorts (pinteraction=0·010)"；"These findings suggest that a hearing intervention might reduce cognitive change over 3 years in populations of older adults at increased risk for cognitive decline but not in populations at decreased risk."

## 5 神经源性膀胱
- <https://doi.org/10.1002/nau.24211> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 31724222 摘要）。标题 "EAU/ESPU guidelines on the management of neurogenic bladder in children and adolescent part I diagnostics and conservative treatment" 匹配。
  - 原文："With conservative treatment the upper urinary tract is preserved in up to 90%, urinary tract infections are common, but not severe, complications of CIC are quite rare and continence can be achieved at adolescence in up to 80% without further treatment."
  - 原文："The transition into adulthood is a complicated time for both patients, their caregivers and doctors, as the patient wants to become independent from caregivers and treatment compliance is reduced."
  - 说明：这是儿童和青少年指南，条目备注里写明了外推边界。

## 6 疼痛与骨质疏松
- <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocn.2025.111660> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 41043376 摘要）。标题 "Prevalence of neuropathic pain following spinal cord injury: An updated systematic review and meta-analysis" 匹配。
  - 原文："24 studies (n = 6,318) with low to moderate risk of bias were included. The pooled prevalence of NP was 57 % (95CI: 51 %, 64 %)"；"Age, gender, traumatic etiology, time since injury, injury severity, acute vs chronic SCI, and NLI did not affect the prevalence rate"。
- <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00198-020-05807-0> — 已打开（PubMed PMID 33386876 摘要）。标题 "The efficacy and safety of bisphosphonate analogs for treatment of osteoporosis after spinal cord injury: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials" 匹配。
  - 原文："nine randomized controlled trials with 206 individuals"；"There were significant differences in BMD of the total hip and lumbar spine or serum C-terminal telopeptide between the bisphosphonate and control groups but no difference in adverse events. The percent change in BMD of the distal femur ... was not superior in the treatment groups."
